## 1. Executive Summary & Foundational Axiom

When conducting AI-assisted computational chemistry research, human effort shifts from mechanical key-typing to **supervisory steering, biophysical verification, code AST auditing, and cognitive deliberation over complex explanations and reasoning traces**.

### The Deliberative Reading Reality
> **Cognitive Deliberation vs. Passive Skimming:** An AI response explaining dielectric shielding ($\epsilon = 4r_{ij}$), MMFF94 force-field parameterization, or Pareto rank dominance cannot be skimmed at typical prose speeds (180–250 WPM). The candidate must **read, pause, critically think through the physical implications, evaluate trade-offs, and formulate the next steering prompt**. Therefore, technical explanations are governed by **deliberative comprehension velocities ($\le 85\text{–}100\text{ WPM}$)**.

### The Bachelor Student Baseline Factor
> **Zero Prior Knowledge Invariant:** The candidate entered the research project with **zero prior formal knowledge of Deep Learning, PyTorch tensor manipulation, Sequence RNN architectures, and RDKit 3D molecular mechanics**. Every concept had to be acquired, digested, and validated from scratch while actively driving the project forward.

## 2. Mathematical Formalization of Cognitive Labor

Total Supervisory Labor Time ($T_{\text{cognitive}}$) is formalized as:

$$T_{\text{cognitive}} = \left( \sum_{i} \frac{W_i}{V_i} + T_{\text{tool\_audit}} \right) \times \Phi_{\text{novice}}$$

Where:
- $W_i$: Empirical word count of category $i$.
- $V_i$: Calibrated comprehension velocity in Words Per Minute (WPM), incorporating cognitive pause & reflection time.
- $T_{\text{tool\_audit}}$: Active inspection of terminal stderr, PyTest tracebacks, and biophysical convergence logs.
- $\Phi_{\text{novice}}$: Novice conceptual assimilation factor ($\Phi_{\text{novice}} = 1.30$, reflecting $+30\%$ literature cross-referencing and glossary lookups).
